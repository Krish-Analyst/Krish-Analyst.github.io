How It Works

The estimate adds up three things: what you pay once to get set up, the stock you buy before your first sale, and the monthly costs you'll have before sales cover them. A buffer goes on top, because almost every launch costs more than planned.

Total = (setup + stock + monthly costs × months) × (1 + buffer %)

Keep It Low

The cheapest launch is a small one. Print-on-demand removes the biggest upfront cost, a free store plan can cover your first months, and your phone takes good enough photos. Start with what you need to make your first sale, and spend more once customers prove the idea works.
