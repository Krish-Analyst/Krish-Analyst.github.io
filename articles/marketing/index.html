Getting Your First Customers Without a Budget

How to use TikTok, Instagram, and direct outreach to build an audience before you spend a single dollar.

May 2026 · 2 min read · By Krish Panda
Key takeaways
  • TikTok has the biggest free reach right now: film your process, not ads.
  • Instagram is where people check you’re real, so keep it clean and consistent.
  • Send real, personal messages and join communities where your customers already are.

Nobody is going to find your store on their own. You have to bring them there. Here's how to do that with zero marketing budget.

TikTok Is the Most Powerful Free Tool Available Right Now

When I posted TikToks for Velora Gran Turismo, some of them hit over 1,000 views with no followers and no paid promotion. That's organic reach you simply cannot get on any other platform right now.

You don't need to be a video expert. Film your process: designing a product, packing an order, showing the brand behind the scenes. Authenticity works better than production quality.

Build an Instagram Presence

Instagram is where people go to check if a brand is real. A clean, consistent feed with good product photos builds trust. Post consistently, use relevant hashtags, and engage with accounts in your niche daily.

Use Direct Outreach

Message people who might care about your product. Not spam. Real messages to real people who fit your audience. Personalized outreach converts far better than random social media posts.

Join Communities Where Your Audience Hangs Out

Reddit, Discord, Facebook Groups: find where your potential customers actually spend time. Contribute value, answer questions, build relationships. Only pitch when it's genuinely relevant.

"The best marketing is when someone else tells people about your business."
