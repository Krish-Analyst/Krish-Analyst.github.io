Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version: Rexyd has no cookies, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. Nothing you type into the free tools ever leaves your browser. The only personal data we collect is your email address, and only if you sign up for the 30-day program. Here is exactly what happens.

Cookies

None. Rexyd doesn't set any cookies, and nothing on the site uses cookies for advertising or tracking. That's why there's no cookie banner.

Visitor Statistics

None. Rexyd doesn't use any analytics or visitor-counting service, so nothing records which pages you visit or where you came from.

The Free Tools

Everything you enter into the Pricing Calculator, Startup Cost Estimator and Discount Calculator is calculated in your own browser. Your numbers are never sent to us or stored anywhere.

What Your Browser Remembers

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your device so the site looks the same next time. The site also keeps an offline copy of its pages in your browser so it loads quickly and works without internet. Neither is sent to us, and you can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

Fonts and Hosting

The font is hosted on rexyd.com itself, so loading the site doesn't contact Google or any font service. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub briefly processes your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to Other Sites

Rexyd links to Instagram, LinkedIn and the tools listed on the Resources page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you click through.

Signing Up for the Program

If you sign up on the homepage, we collect your email address, the date you signed up and that you ticked the box confirming you're 13 or older and happy to get emails. That's all.

We use your email only to send you updates and weekly tasks for the Rexyd 30-day program. We never sell it, share it or use it for advertising. It's stored in a private Google Sheet that only Rexyd can open, using Google's secure servers (see Google's privacy policy).

The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email or by writing to contact@rexyd.com, and we'll delete your email. We also delete sign-ups from a group six months after it ends unless you sign up again.

You need to be 13 or older to sign up yourself, which is the age of digital consent in Denmark.

Email

If you email contact@rexyd.com, your address and message are used only to reply to you. You won't be added to any mailing list.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. The only personal data we might have is your sign-up email, or an email you sent us. Write to contact@rexyd.com and we'll sort it out. You can also contact the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Who Runs Rexyd

Rexyd is run by Krish Panda in Denmark. If this page changes, the date at the top will change too.
